## Color Palette
- Background: `0 0% 4%` (#0A0A0B — near black)
- Card: `0 0% 7%`
- Primary: `160 84% 39%` (#10B981 — emerald)
- Muted: neutral grays
- Destructive: red `0 62% 55%`
- Warning: amber `38 92% 50%`
- No blue, purple, or golden colors

## Effects
- `.glass` — frosted glass with border + blur
- `.glow` / `.glow-sm` — emerald glow shadows
- `.gradient-text` — emerald gradient text
- `.dot-grid` — subtle dot pattern background
- `.glow-line` — horizontal gradient separator

## Animations
- AI thinking dots (wave pattern)
- Shimmer for skeleton loaders
- Scroll reveal (framer-motion)
- Page transitions (framer-motion)
- Micro-interactions on hover
